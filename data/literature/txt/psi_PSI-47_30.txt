2.010 DAFT P-TRAK AND DUSTTRAK SETUP
MGUEEXPRSDAFTN010



                                                                                      Start_IMS


OBJECTIVE:
Dust and Aerosol measurement Feasibility Test (DAFT). This procedure assembles the P-Trak
and DustTrak. It prepares the P-Trak and DustTrak for future sample collection.

STATION PARTS:
DustTrak               P/N DAFT-0201
P-Trak-01              P/N DAFT-0101
DAFT Accessories Kit         P/N DAFT-0700
P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01         P/N DAFT-0601-1
P-Trak Batteries [6]         P/N DAFT-0708
Used P-Trak Batteries        P/N DAFT-0718
DustTrak Batteries [4]       P/N DAFT-0709
Used DustTrak Batteries      P/N DAFT-0719


                                                    NOTE
                     1. This procedure contains Near Real-Time video downlink or LAB VTR
                        recording requirements.

                     2. AOS required for POIC to configure onboard system for downlink or
                        LAB VTR recording.

              1.    P-TRAK ASSEMBLY

                     1.1    Unstow:
                                 P-Trak-01
                                 P-Trak Batteries(Used P-Trak Batteries) (six)
                                 DAFT Accessories Kit

                            Tmpry stow:
                                P-Trak Batteries(Used P-Trak Batteries) (six)
                                DAFT Accessories Kit

                                    Start Video Activity

                     1.2    If video is required,
                               Perform {P/TV 584 DAFT}.

P-Trak-01            1.3    Remove and Tmpry stow P-Trak Battery Cover.

                     1.4    Retrieve P-Trak Batteries(Used P-Trak Batteries) (six).

             2.    ALCOHOL WICK INSERTION

                   2.1    Unstow P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01.

                   2.2    Retrieve P-Trak-01.

                                                     CAUTION
                           To reduce the amount of alcohol released into ISS,
                           performance of the Alcohol Wick Insertion (steps 2.3 to 2.7)
                           must be limited to one minute.

P-Trak-01          2.3    Remove For Storage Only Cap (turn 1/4 turn CCW).
                          Tmpry stow For Storage Only Cap.

                   2.4    On P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01, remove
                           Alcohol Wick Cap.
                          Tmpry stow Alcohol Wick Cap.

                   2.5    Remove Alcohol Wick from P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01
                            (turn 1/4 turn CCW).
                          Tmpry stow P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01.
                          Insert Alcohol Wick into P-Trak-01 using the alignment pin
                            (turn 1/4 turn CW).

                2.6    Retrieve P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01.
                       Retrieve and insert For Storage Only Cap into P-Trak Alcohol
                        Wick Container-01 using the alignment pin (turn 1/4 turn CW).

                2.7    Retrieve and replace Alcohol Wick Cap on P-Trak Alcohol Wick
                        Container-01 (finger tighten).

                2.8    Stow or Tmpry stow: per Execution Notes
                            P-Trak-01
                            P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01

                                                NOTE
                 DustTrak Xµm Adapter will be used for DustTrak 10µm Adapter,
                 DustTrak 2.5µm Adapter or DustTrak 1µm Adapter.

           3.   DUSTTRAK ASSEMBLY

                3.1    Unstow:
                            DustTrak
                            DustTrak Batteries(Used DustTrak Batteries) (four)

                        Tmpry stow DustTrak Batteries(Used DustTrak Batteries) (four).

DustTrak        3.2     Remove (unscrew) and Tmpry stow DustTrak Battery Cover.
                        Retrieve DustTrak Batteries(Used DustTrak Batteries) (four).

                                                   NOTE
                        Move DustTrak battery pack wire to prevent obstruction of the
                        DustTrak battery pack.

                3.3    Remove DustTrak battery pack from DustTrak (slight prying
                         motion).
                       Install DustTrak Batteries(Used DustTrak Batteries) (four)
                         (per marked orientation).

                                                     NOTE
                        1. DustTrak battery pack wire should cover the DustTrak
                           battery pack after replacement of DustTrak battery pack.

                        2. DustTrak Battery Cover must be properly inserted into the
                           DustTrak for secure closure.

                        3. DustTrak Battery Cover closure is spring loaded. Slight
                           pressure is needed to secure DustTrak Battery Cover.

                3.4    Replace DustTrak battery pack in DustTrak.

                3.5    Retrieve and replace (hand tighten) DustTrak Battery Cover
                        taking care not to pinch DustTrak battery pack wire.

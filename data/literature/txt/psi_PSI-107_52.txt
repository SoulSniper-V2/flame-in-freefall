SUBJECT:       SPICE Flow Duct Velocity Measurements

DATE:          July 6, 2009
______________________________________________________________

This Memo addresses the SPICE flow duct velocity measurements, which were performed by Kevin
Magee on the flight hardware from June 16-24, 2008. A hot wire anemometer probe was used to
measure the air velocity at various points within the flow duct. This probe was passed into the duct
through a special multi-piece top window with probe ports at the desired locations. Unused ports were
taped shut on the inside and outside to prevent leakage and minimize flow disturbances. The window
itself was assembled and sealed with Kapton tape around its perimeter.

The probe used was a TSI, Inc., Model 1261A miniature boundary layer probe (S/N# 012077) with a
T1.5 (coated tungsten platinum) wire and a 56 ohm control resistor to give a 260oC operating
temperature. Its calibration was performed in a free jet facility on April 28, 2006.

Measurements were taken at two axial stations of 13 and 56 mm as measured by the permanent scale
bonded to the floor of the duct. These locations are where the tip of the probe was located, not where
the ports were located since the probe curves forward into the air flow. At each axial station,
measurements were taken at three width locations: the duct centerline, 20 mm off the centerline
towards the back wall (called the “inside” position), and 20 mm off the centerline towards the front
window (called the “outside” position). At each width position, measurements were taken at three
heights: 19, 36, and 57 mm off the floor of the duct. It should be noted that the 36 mm height
corresponds to the height of the nozzle, which was installed for the tests. However, no flow out of the
nozzle was done during the tests. All locations are shown in Figure 1.

At each measured position, the anemometer probe’s velocity readings were recorded for FAN dial
settings of 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10. The FAN and AIR readings were also noted. Plots were then created
to compare the probe velocity, AIR reading, and FAN reading for the different FAN dial settings.
Velocity profile plots were also generated at the various axial stations in both width and height. These
plots and the raw data can be found in the spreadsheets at the following location.

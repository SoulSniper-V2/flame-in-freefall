                                       Electric Field (1.7)
    •     Voltage range         -10 to +10 kV set within ±0.1% of set point
    •     Step change            any polarity and magnitude within range
                                ≤5 ms rise time
    •     Alternating polarity  sequence of steps at 0-100 Hz (mimic AC field)
    •     Uplink                voltage, polarity, step changes, timing
    •     No conductive contact mesh and burner (except for temp. measurement)
    •     Conductive free zone ≥30 mm more than mesh diameter
                                from burner to ≥20 mm downstream of mesh
    •     Current leakage       <100 nanoamps (at ±10 kV with a 35-mm spacing)
    •     Removable             high voltage power supplies, associated electronics

                           Ambient Environment (1.11)
    • Composition            uplinkable and variable to ±0.01 mole fraction
    • Contaminants           ≤0.01 mole fraction and dry, unless waived
    • Atmosphere             well mixed
    • Quiescent              300 s hold period before ignition
    • Pressure               0.1 to 3 bar within ±3% of set point
    • Temperature            290-310 K, uniform within ±5 K
    • Free volume            ≥80 liters, symmetric as possible
    • Free zone
       – spherical flames    ≥200 mm diameter
       – non-spherical       ≥150 mm diameter cylinder, ≥200 mm up, etc.
    • Disturbance limit      burner flow, combustion, probe motion, g-jitter
    • No test prohibition    during thruster firing, docking, or undocking
    • ‘Black’ background     no reflection or emission in view of sensor
    • Emissivity             ≥0.5 except for burners, probe tips, igniter, etc.
    Req’t for dynamic venting was disallowed because of difficulty & cost!

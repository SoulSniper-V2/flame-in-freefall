            SLICE SUCCESS CRITERIA
• Minimum Success -– Still images are acquired for at least 15
  different lifted flames with different combinations of fuel flow and air
  velocity with a single fuel, or at least 10 different lifted flames each
  of two fuels, or at least 5 different lifted flames each of 3 or more
  fuels.
• High Success -– Still images are acquired of at least 30 different
  lifted flames total from at least 3 different fuels. The still images are
  acquired with a blackbody characterized camera for soot pyrometry
  or liftoff limits are determined for at least one fuel.
• Complete Success -– Testing conducted with all 12 fuel bottles, with
  identification of stability limits and smoke points (within the range of
  the SPICE velocity limits). Still images are taken of lifted flames with
  a blackbody characterized camera.

OBJECTIVE:
Dust and Aerosol measurement Feasibility Test (DAFT). This procedure utilizes an ambient air
sample from ISS for measurement by the P-Trak and DustTrak. It initiates the P-Trak and
DustTrak for sampling and shutdown.

STATION PARTS:
DAFT Accessories Kit       P/N DAFT-0700
DustTrak             P/N DAFT-0201
P-Trak-01            P/N DAFT-0101
Single Strap P/N PERS0041
Cable Straps [6]           P/N 528-43074-1


                                                     NOTE
                      1. DAFT P-TRAK AND DUSTTRAK SAMPLING alternates between
                         both the P-Trak and DustTrak hardware units.

                      2. This procedure contains Near Real-Time video downlink or LAB VTR
                         recording requirements.

                      3. AOS required for POIC to configure onboard system for downlink or
                         LAB VTR recording.

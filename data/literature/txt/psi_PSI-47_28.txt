OBJECTIVE:
Dust and Aerosol measurement Feasibility Test (DAFT). This procedure disassembles and
stows the P-Trak and DustTrak. It completes the DAFT sample collection process.

P-Trak-01      P/N DAFT-0101

Used P-Trak Batteries      P/N DAFT-0718
DustTrak Batteries [4]     P/N DAFT-0709
Used DustTrak Batteries    P/N DAFT-0719
Single Strap P/N PERS0041
Cable Straps [6]           P/N 528-43074-1

STATION TOOLS:
4X4 Ziplock Bag [2]         P/N 528-21039-2
Fine Point Sharpie          P/N 528-40674-1
Bar Code Kit                P/N 582-21046-3

                      Move DustTrak battery pack wire to prevent obstruction of the

               2.9    Remove DustTrak battery pack from DustTrak (slight prying
                       motion).

               2.10   Remove DustTrak Batteries (four).
                      Tmpry stow DustTrak Batteries (four).

                                                    NOTE
                       1. DustTrak battery pack wire should cover the DustTrak
                          battery pack after replacement of DustTrak battery pack.

                       2. DustTrak Battery Cover must be properly inserted into the
                          DustTrak for secure closure.

                       3. DustTrak Battery Cover closure is spring loaded. Slight
                          pressure is needed to secure DustTrak Battery Cover.

               2.11   Replace DustTrak battery pack in DustTrak.

               2.12   Retrieve and replace DustTrak Battery Cover taking care not to
                       pinch DustTrak battery pack wire.

               2.13   Stow DustTrak.

                              Fine Point Sharpie
                              4X4 Ziplock Bag

                       Used DustTrak Batteries P/N DAFT-0719.

 13.     ABSTRACT                  (Maximum           200     words)



           Research                in microgravity                  ('low-gravity)             combustion               promises              innovations                   and improvements                        in fire prevention                        and
           response                for human-crew                   spacecraft.           Findings           indicate            that material                    flammability                 and fire spread                 in microgravity                      are
           significantly                  affected          by atmospheric                 flow      rate,     oxygen            concentration,                          and diluent           composition.                This        information                  can        lead
           to modifications                       and correlations                 to standard            material-assessment                                tests       for prediction              of fire       resistance                in space.             Research
           on smoke-particle                         changes           in microgravity                promises            future         improvements                         and increased                 sensitivity               of smoke              detectors             in
           spacecraft.                 Research             on fire suppression                 by extinguishing                       agents               and      venting      can yield             new       information                  on effective
           control           of the rare,             but serious            fire events          in spacecraft.

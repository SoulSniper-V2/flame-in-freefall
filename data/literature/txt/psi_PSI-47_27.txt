OBJECTIVE:
Dust and Aerosol measurement Feasibility Test (DAFT). This procedure transfers the data from
the P-Trak and DustTrak after sample collection has been completed.

STATION PARTS:
DAFT Accessories Kit        P/N DAFT-0700
DustTrak             P/N DAFT-0201
P-Trak-01            P/N DAFT-0101
P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01 P/N DAFT-0601-1
ELC


               1.    ALCOHOL WICK REMOVAL

                      1.1    Unstow P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01.

                      1.2    Retrieve P-Trak-01.

                      1.3   √P-Trak-01 power − Off

                                                         CAUTION
                               To reduce the amount of alcohol released into ISS,
                               performance of the Alcohol Wick Removal (steps 1.4 to 1.8)
                               must be limited to one minute.

                      1.4    On P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01, remove Alcohol Wick
                                Cap.
                             Tmpry stow Alcohol Wick Cap.

                      1.5    On P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01, remove For Storage Only
                              Cap (turn 1/4 turn CCW).

                             Tmpry stow:
                                 For Storage Only Cap
                                 P-Trak Alcohol Wick Container-01

4.001 SPICE VIDEO OVERLAY RESET
(mgolall000251/IMPACT)                  Page 1 of 13 pages

OBJECTIVE:
Reset the SPICE Power/Video Unit firmware to return the SPICE display to normal (NOR)
 mode in support of SLICE (Structure and Liftoff in Combustion Experiment). To do this, the
 Power/Video Unit will be removed from MSG so the unit’s Video Titler Box can be opened to
 scrape off conformal coating from circuit board pins (five of ten) to make temporary jumper
 connections for advancing through the setup menu.
TOOLS:
8.5" Handrail, if needed
3" Common Tip Screwdriver, if needed
4 mm Hex Head, 1/4" Drive
Driver Handle, 1/4" Drive
Vacuum Cleaner
Fiberglass Stick P/N 182SO020 or Leatherman Tool, crew preference
ISS Pin Kit P/N SJG33110644-301
  Pin to Socket Test Jumper Lead, 24 in. P/N SEG33112714-307 (five)
Static Wrist Tether
Fine Point Sharpie
D2Xs Camera
MATERIALS:
Kapton Tape
4X4 Ziplock Bag or equivalent

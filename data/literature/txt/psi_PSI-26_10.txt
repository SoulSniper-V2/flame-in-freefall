            SLICE SCIENCE OVERVIEW
OBJECTIVES
• Identify the lift-off velocity limits in air
  for flames of methane, ethylene, and
  selected dilutions of each fuel as a
  function of the nozzle size. When
  possible, also identify the blow-out
  velocities (i.e., when the flame
  extinguishes).
• Characterize the hysteresis of flame
  lifting and reattachment (i.e.,
  stabilization) with variations in flow
  conditions as a function of the fuel and
  nozzle size.
• Characterize the structure of the flame,
  especially its base (i.e., stabilizing
  region), from attached through lifted
  conditions as a function of the fuel,
  nozzle size, and flow conditions.

       SLICE SCIENCE REQUIREMENTS
•   Fuel Nozzles
      – All nozzles are replaceable on-orbit by the crew
      – Fuel nozzle installed orientation is in the axis of the flow duct
      – Fuel nozzles are circular with a constant internal cross section
      – 2 new nozzles have been fabricated.
      – Off-the-shelf tubes have inner diameter with a tolerance of better than the required
        ±0.1 mm
      – The original SPICE nozzles (0.4, 0.8, and 1.6 mm) and the new SLICE nozzles will be
        measured such that the actual size is known with an accuracy of ±0.05 mm
      – Burner tubes are made of 304 Stainless Steel
•   FUELS
      – Project has procured gases with appropriate fuel type, purity, and concentration such
        that the gas is dry with a net amount of contaminants less than a mole and
        concentration known to within ±0.005 mole fraction as verified by vendor certification
        documentation.
      – Gas volume (bottle pressure) has been maximized based on the minimum thermal
        environment such that condensation does not occur.
      – The 12 fuels bottles will be filled as shown in Slide 6.
•   Oxygen Concentration
      – Project will include steps in crew procedures to install a Compound Specific Analyzer
        (CSA-O2) into the MSG to measure the Oxygen concentration. There is also a Major
        Constituent Analyzer available to measure the Columbus Module concentration.

            SLICE SUCCESS CRITERIA
• Minimum Success -– Still images are acquired for at least 15
  different lifted flames with different combinations of fuel flow
  and air velocity with a single fuel, or at least 10 different lifted
  flames each of two fuels, or at least 5 different lifted flames
  each of 3 or more fuels.
• High Success -– Still images are acquired of at least 30
  different lifted flames total from at least 3 different fuels. The
  still images are acquired with a blackbody characterized
  camera for soot pyrometry or liftoff limits are determined for at
  least one fuel.
• Complete Success -– Testing conducted with all 12 fuel
  bottles, with identification of stability limits and smoke points
  (within the range of the SPICE velocity limits). Still images
  are taken of lifted flames with a blackbody characterized
  camera.

                     BASS Science Overview
Objective:
Bridge the gap between normal gravity NASA‐STD‐6001 Test # 1 method, ground based microgravity
tests, and actual material flammability in microgravity.

Test effectiveness of an inert, gaseous extinguishing agent (similar to that used on
ISS) in putting out flames over different materials, geometries, and flow.

Relevance/Impact:
Relationship between ground test and microgravity test can be investigated by observing flames burning
for longer times.

Practical, realistic fuels in typical geometries will be examined, including wake flames which
have characteristics of a fire burning behind another object.

Development Approach:
BASS will utilize the on orbit hardware SPICE which was launched on STS‐126 and operates on ISS.

Crew required to set up and operate the experiment. Video and data down‐linked to the ground for
evaluation.

       BASS SCIENCE REQUIREMENTS
•   General Requirements
      – Each Solid Fuel Assembly is mounted inside the Experiment Assembly via a
        magnetic interface such that it can be installed and removed by the crew
      – Solid fuel ignition will be done with a hot-wire (Kanthol)
      – Nitrogen gas to be used instead of fuel from 0 to 500 SCCM N2. Instead of
        connecting the Experiment Assembly to the gas bottle it is connected via the
        GN2 hose to the MSG GN2 interface.
      – 3.2 mm normal length and 3.2 mm short length burner tubes will be
        manifested
      – The Control Box will be modified to allow the fan flow adjustment to stop the fan
        when set to its minimum as a safety mechanism to extinguish flame.
•   Parameter Measurement
      – Measurement of the GN2 Flow rate, Fan Voltage, Co-Flow velocity, and
        flame radiation will be measured and overlaid on the Video.
      – The acquisition rate as measured by SPICE hardware is 4 Hz.
      – The measurement accuracy of ±10% was met by the SPICE hardware prior
        to its launch.
•   Imaging Requirements
      – The Field of view of the video can not be changed from SPICE.
      – The Still Image requirements are the same as for SPICE these were met.
        Enhanced capability is expected with the D300s.
